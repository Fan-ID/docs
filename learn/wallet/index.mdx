title: Credit wallet
description: Prepaid workspace balance you use to fund Soundlink campaigns without paying by card for every cycle.
---

The **credit wallet** is a prepaid balance in US dollars for your **workspace** (organization). You add credits by topping up, then spend them when you launch or renew campaigns.

Credits live at the workspace level — they are shared by everyone in the organization, not tied to a single user account.

## Where to find it

Open **Settings → Credit wallet** in your workspace. You will see:

- **Current balance** — available credits right now
- **Transaction history** — top-ups, deductions, and refunds with running balance

You can also see your balance when creating a campaign or managing a wallet-funded campaign from campaign insights.

## What you can do with credits

| Action | Summary |
| --- | --- |
| [Top up](/learn/wallet/top-up) | Buy credits with a one-time card payment |
| [Pay for a campaign](/learn/wallet/paying-for-campaigns) | Fund a full cycle from the wallet, or apply credits as a discount at Stripe checkout |
| [Renew automatically](/learn/wallet/renewals) | Wallet campaigns can renew each cycle if auto-renew is on and balance is sufficient |
| [Increase campaign budget](/learn/wallet/campaign-budget-top-up) | Spend credits to raise daily budget on an active wallet campaign |
| [Stop a campaign](/learn/wallet/limitations-and-edge-cases#stopping-a-wallet-campaign) | Unused credits for the current cycle can return to your wallet |

## Payment options at checkout

When you create a paid growth campaign, Soundlink offers:

1. **Pay with card** — Stripe subscription for the campaign cycle. You can optionally apply wallet credits as a partial discount; the remainder is charged to your card.
2. **Pay with wallet** — Available when your balance covers the **full first cycle** total. No card charge for that cycle.

You must have enough balance for the option you choose. See [Paying for campaigns](/learn/wallet/paying-for-campaigns) for details.
