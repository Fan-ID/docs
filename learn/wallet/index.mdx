---
title: Credit wallet
description: Prepaid workspace balance you use to fund Soundlink campaigns without paying by card for every cycle.
---

The **credit wallet** is a prepaid balance in US dollars for your **workspace** (organization). You add credits by topping up, then spend them when you launch or renew campaigns.

Credits live at the workspace level — they are shared by everyone in the organization, not tied to a single user account. The Credit Wallet is available to every Soundlink organization.

## Where to find it

Open **Settings → Credit wallet & payment methods**. This page combines:

- **Current balance** — available credits right now, with the date last updated
- **Saved payment methods** — cards and other methods saved to your workspace
- **Auto-recharge** — settings for automatically topping up your wallet
- **Upcoming renewals** — wallet-funded campaigns renewing in the next 7 days
- **Activity** — a full log of top-ups and deductions, with the date, event, related campaign (if any), amount, and running balance after each transaction

You can also see your wallet balance when creating a new campaign, or on a wallet-funded campaign's row in your dashboard.

## What you can do with credits

| Action | Summary |
| --- | --- |
| [Top up](/learn/wallet/top-up) | Add credits with a one-time payment from a saved payment method |
| [Save payment methods](/learn/wallet/payment-methods) | Store cards (and other methods) for top-ups and auto-recharge |
| [Auto-recharge](/learn/wallet/auto-recharge) | Automatically top up when balance is short for renewals |
| [Pay for a campaign](/learn/wallet/paying-for-campaigns) | Fund the campaign cycle from the wallet (top up inline if balance is short) |
| [Renew automatically](/learn/wallet/renewals) | Wallet campaigns renew each cycle if auto-renew is on and balance is sufficient (or auto-recharge covers the gap) |
| [Manage campaign budget](/learn/wallet/campaign-budget-top-up) | Raise or lower daily budget on an active wallet campaign |
| [Stop a campaign](/learn/wallet/limitations-and-edge-cases#stopping-a-wallet-campaign) | Unused credits for the current cycle return to your wallet |

## Paying for a new campaign

New paid growth campaigns use a **single wallet-first payment** on the Review step:

1. Soundlink shows your wallet balance and the first-cycle total.
2. If balance covers the total, create debits the wallet and launches the campaign.
3. If balance is short, use **Top up wallet** on Review (add a **backup card** first if you do not have one yet).

There is no separate “pay with Stripe subscription” option in this flow. Saved cards are for topping up the wallet and for auto-recharge on renewals. See [Paying for campaigns](/learn/wallet/paying-for-campaigns).
