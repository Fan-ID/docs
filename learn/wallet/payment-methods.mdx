title: Payment methods

You can save multiple payment methods to your workspace. Soundlink uses the **active** method for manual top-ups and for [auto-recharge](/learn/wallet/auto-recharge).

## Where to manage them

Go to **Settings → Credit wallet & payment methods**. The **Saved payment methods** section lists every method on your workspace.

## Labels

Each saved method is labeled as either:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | Currently used for top-ups and auto-recharge |
| **Inactive** | Saved but not currently in use |

Only one payment method can be **Active** at a time.

## What you see on each method

- **Cards** — last four digits and expiration date
- **PayPal** — linked email address (when available)

## Add a payment method

1. Click **Add payment method**.
2. Choose **Card** to add a card, or **Pay with more methods** for Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, and similar options.
3. Complete the Stripe setup flow.

If the card form or other methods do not appear, use **Pay without Link** when Stripe shows that option.

After setup succeeds, the new method appears in the list. If you had no active method, Soundlink typically activates the new one and can enable [auto-recharge](/learn/wallet/auto-recharge) for your workspace.

## Manage an existing method

Use the **⋯** menu next to a saved method to:

- Set it as your **active** method
- Set it as **inactive**
- **Remove** it from the workspace

<Warning>
  If you deactivate or remove your only active payment method, Soundlink turns **auto-recharge** off until you activate another method.
</Warning>

## Detached or expired methods

If Stripe removes a method (for example, the card is detached or expires), Soundlink marks it so it cannot be reactivated. Add a new payment method instead.
