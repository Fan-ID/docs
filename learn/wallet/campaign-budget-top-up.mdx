---
title: Campaign budget top-up
description: Increase daily budget on an active wallet campaign by spending credits from your wallet.
---

A **campaign budget top-up** increases how much your wallet campaign spends per day. It is separate from **wallet top-up** (adding credits to your workspace balance).

You use credits already in your wallet — there is no new Stripe payment for this action.

## Where to do it

From **campaign insights** (or the campaign row on your dashboard) for a **wallet** campaign, use **Add budget** when the campaign is **active**. The app shows your current wallet balance in context.

This only works for campaigns originally created with the wallet. Card-funded campaigns are topped up via card instead — the two payment modes cannot be mixed.

## How it works

1. You choose how much extra budget to add (total dollar amount for the top-up).
2. Soundlink splits that amount across **remaining days** in the current cycle to compute additional daily budget.
3. Credits are **deducted from your wallet**.
4. Budget changes are applied to your Meta campaign (processing happens asynchronously after the debit is recorded).

If the Meta update cannot be applied, Soundlink may **refund** the debit to your wallet.

## Top-up modes

When increasing budget, you can choose how the change applies:

| Mode | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Current cycle only** | Raises budget for the rest of this cycle; debits your wallet |
| **Next renewal only** | Sets a higher daily budget starting at the next cycle; **no wallet debit** for the current cycle |
| **Current cycle and renewals** | Raises budget now and keeps the increase for future cycles; debits your wallet for the current-cycle portion |

The app labels these options in plain language during the flow.

## Requirements and limits

Soundlink blocks campaign budget top-up when:

- Campaign is not **active**
- Campaign is not **wallet** payment mode
- **Two or fewer days** remain in the current cycle
- Top-up would add **less than $1 per day** on average (`total amount ÷ remaining days` must be at least $1)
- Wallet balance is **insufficient** for the debit (current-cycle modes)

## After a successful top-up

- Your wallet balance decreases by the debited amount.
- Ledger shows a **deduct** for the campaign.
- Daily budget on the campaign reflects the increase once Meta processing completes.

If processing is delayed, the debit is still recorded; support can help if budget does not update as expected.
