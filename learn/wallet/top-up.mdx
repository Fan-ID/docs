Top up adds credits to your workspace balance through **Stripe Checkout**. Each top-up is a separate one-time payment — it is not a subscription.

[Insert image here]

## How to top up

1. Go to **Settings → Credit wallet**.
2. Click **Top up** (or the equivalent action on the balance card).
3. Choose a preset amount or enter a custom amount.
4. Complete payment in Stripe Checkout.
5. After success, you return to the Credit wallet page and your balance updates.

Preset amounts in the app are **$100**, **$250**, **$500**, and **$1,000**. You can also enter a custom amount within the limits below.

## Amount limits

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum | **$50** |
| Maximum | **$100,000** |

Amounts outside this range cannot proceed to checkout.

## After payment

- Stripe may calculate tax automatically depending on your billing details.
- An invoice can be generated for the payment.
- Credits appear in your ledger as a **topup** event.
- If you have wallet campaigns in **renew failed** status with **auto-renew still on**, Soundlink may retry renewal after your balance increases.

<Note>
  Top-up credits are for your workspace only. They cannot be transferred to another workspace or withdrawn as cash.
</Note>

## Cancelled checkout

If you cancel Stripe Checkout, no credits are added and your balance stays the same. You can start a new top-up at any time.

## Failed verification

If you return from checkout but payment verification fails, the Credit wallet page shows a failure state. Your balance is unchanged until payment succeeds. Try topping up again or contact support if the problem continues.
