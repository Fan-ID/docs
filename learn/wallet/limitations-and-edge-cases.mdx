This page collects product limits and non-obvious behavior confirmed in Soundlink today.

## Wallet top-up limits

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum top-up | $50 |
| Maximum top-up | $100,000 |
| Currency | USD |
| Transfer | Credits cannot be moved to another workspace or cashed out |
| Expiry | Credits do not expire |

## Payment methods and auto-recharge

- Only one payment method can be **Active** per workspace.
- Auto-recharge requires an active payment method.
- Deactivating or removing the last active method turns auto-recharge **off**.
- Detached or expired methods cannot be reactivated — add a new method.
- Monthly cap (optional) limits auto-charges per **calendar month**.
- Auto-recharge does not turn on only because you saved a card — you enable it (or Soundlink enables it when you add a first active method in some flows).

## Pay with wallet at creation

- Balance must cover **100%** of the first cycle total (`daily budget × duration`).
- Partial balance alone is **not** enough for full wallet payment — use card checkout with partial credit instead, or [top up](/learn/wallet/top-up) from the Review step.

## Partial credit on card checkout

- Maximum applicable credit: `min(wallet balance, cycle total)`.
- Credits reserve **immediately** when you apply them.
- Abandoned checkout: discount expires in about **one hour**; reserved credits are refunded to your wallet shortly after expiry (usually within a few minutes, not necessarily the instant the hour ends).
- UI copy may mention ~60 minutes; the discount expires after about **one hour**.

## Campaign budget top-up

- Wallet campaigns only, **active** status
- Requires **more than two days** left in the cycle
- Minimum **$1/day** effective increase
- **Next renewal only** mode does not debit the wallet for the current cycle
- Cannot mix wallet and card funding on the same campaign

## Stopping a wallet campaign

You can stop a wallet campaign when status is **active** or **renew failed**.

- Soundlink pauses ads on connected platforms.
- **Unused credits** for the current cycle return to your wallet based on actual Meta spend.
- UI copy: refunds typically appear within a few minutes.
- Stop is blocked while status is **creating** or **renewing** — try again shortly.

Stopped and ended campaigns cannot be stopped again.

## Refunds are wallet credits only

When you stop a campaign early, unused budget for the current cycle is returned as **Credit Wallet** balance. As of **June 16, 2026**, monetary (cash) refunds for stopped campaigns are no longer available per Soundlink's Terms & Conditions.

## End-of-campaign refunds

When a Meta campaign **ends** (natural end of cycle or lifecycle), Soundlink may refund **unused net spend** to your wallet if:

- Campaign status is **ended**
- Platform is **Meta**
- No stop refund was already recorded for that campaign
- Calculated refund amount is greater than zero

Refund timing depends on spend data availability from Meta.

## Renew failed

| Topic | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Cause | Insufficient wallet balance at renewal (and auto-recharge did not cover the gap) |
| User action | Top up; check auto-recharge and active payment method; confirm auto-renew is still on |
| Recovery | Automatic retry after balance increases, only if **auto-renew is still on** |
| Email | Insufficient-credit notification to campaign creator when renewal fails |

## Auto-renew toggle (wallet)

Editable while the campaign is **active** and the cycle end date is still in the future.

Disabled when the campaign is **stopped**, **ended**, **creating**, **renewing**, or **past the cycle end date** — including most **renew failed** campaigns, where the due date has already passed and the toggle is no longer available in the app.

## Workspace plan (upgrade / downgrade)

- Plan tier changes **Ad fees**, which affects **renewal debits** and **campaign budget top-up** math.
- Plan changes do **not** automatically credit or debit your wallet balance.

## Campaign statuses to know

| Status | Wallet relevance |
| --- | --- |
| **active** | Normal operation; top-up and stop available |
| **renewing** | Renewal in progress; wait before stopping |
| **renew_failed** | Needs top-up (or successful auto-recharge); stop still available |
| **stopped** | Terminal; no further wallet charges for new cycles |
| **ended** | Cycle finished; end refund may apply |
| **creating** | Setup in progress; wallet payment already debited for wallet creates |
