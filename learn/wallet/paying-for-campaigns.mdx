When you finish campaign setup, the review step shows how you will pay for the **first cycle**. Your daily budget and campaign duration determine the cycle total shown in the app.

## Cycle total at creation

For the first cycle at checkout, the total is:

**Daily budget × campaign duration (days)**

This is the amount Soundlink uses to decide whether you can pay fully from the wallet or how much you can apply as a partial discount.

<Note>
  **Renewals** use a calculation that includes your workspace plan's **Ad fees**. The renewal amount can differ from the first-cycle total shown at creation. See [Renewals](/learn/wallet/renewals).
</Note>

## Option 1: Pay with card (Stripe)

This is the default. Soundlink creates a **Stripe subscription** for the campaign cycle and charges your card.

### Apply wallet credits as a discount

On the card payment option, you can apply part of your wallet balance to reduce what you pay on the card:

- You can apply up to **the lesser of your balance and the cycle total**.
- The selected amount is **deducted from your wallet immediately** and converted into a Stripe checkout discount.
- You pay the **remainder on your card**.

If you leave Stripe Checkout without completing payment:

- The discount expires after about **one hour**.
- Unused reserved credits are **refunded** to your wallet shortly after expiry (usually within a few minutes).

<Warning>
  Do not close checkout after applying credits unless you intend to abandon the payment. Credits are reserved as soon as you apply them.
</Warning>

You cannot combine wallet credit discount with other coupon types on the same checkout in the way the app enforces mutual exclusion — selecting a traditional coupon clears wallet credit selection.

## Option 2: Pay with wallet

Choose **Pay with wallet** when your balance covers the **entire first cycle total**.

- The full cycle amount is deducted from your wallet when the campaign is created.
- No Stripe subscription is created for that cycle's upfront payment.
- If balance is insufficient, this option is disabled and you must top up or pay by card.

Requirements shown in the app:

- Balance must be **greater than or equal to** the cycle total.

## Insufficient balance

If you try to pay with wallet without enough credits, Soundlink blocks creation and shows how much you need versus what you have. Top up from **Settings → Credit wallet** or switch to card payment (with or without partial credit).

## After the campaign is live

- **Wallet campaigns** — ongoing spend and renewals draw from your wallet. See [Renewals](/learn/wallet/renewals).
- **Card campaigns** — renewals follow your Stripe subscription and auto-renew settings for subscription-backed campaigns.
