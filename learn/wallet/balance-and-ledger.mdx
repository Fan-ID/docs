Your **current balance** is the amount of credits available to spend right now. Every change — top-up, campaign charge, or refund — is recorded in your **ledger** (transaction history).

## Balance basics

- Balance is stored **per workspace**, not per user.
- Amounts are in **US dollars**, shown with two decimal places.
- The balance on the Credit wallet page updates after successful top-ups, campaign charges, and refunds.

If the page cannot load balance or history, you may see a partial-error banner. Retry refreshing the page or contact support if the issue persists.

## Ledger events

Each row in transaction history includes a date, event type, amount, and **balance after** that transaction.

| Event type | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **topup** | Credits added to your wallet (for example, after a successful top-up checkout) |
| **deduct** | Credits spent (campaign allocation, budget top-up, or credit reserved for a Stripe checkout discount) |
| **refund** | Credits returned (unused spend after stop/end, expired checkout discount, or reversed campaign top-up) |

Campaign-linked rows may include a link to the related campaign in insights when available.

## What reduces balance

Credits are deducted when you:

- **Create a wallet campaign** — the full first cycle is charged upfront (`daily budget × campaign duration`).
- **Apply credits at Stripe checkout** — the amount you choose is reserved immediately and applied as a discount on the card payment.
- **Top up an active wallet campaign's budget** — credits pay for the budget increase (see [Campaign budget top-up](/learn/wallet/campaign-budget-top-up)).

## What increases balance

Credits are added when you:

- **Complete a wallet top-up** — Stripe checkout succeeds and credits are applied to your workspace.
- **Stop or end a campaign with unused spend** — unspent credits for the cycle can return to your wallet based on actual ad spend.
- **Abandon a Stripe checkout after applying credits** — reserved credits are refunded after the checkout discount expires (typically within about an hour).
- **Reverse an unapplied campaign budget top-up** — if a budget top-up could not be applied, credits may be refunded.

## Workspace plan and Ad fees

Your Soundlink **workspace plan** includes **Ad fees** used when calculating some wallet charges — especially **renewals** and **campaign budget top-ups**. The fee does not change your stored balance when you upgrade or downgrade a plan; it affects how much is **debited** on those operations.

See [Renewals](/learn/wallet/renewals) for how fees apply on renewal cycles.
