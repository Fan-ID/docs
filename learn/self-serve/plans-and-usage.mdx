Self-Serve bills as a **fixed monthly subscription**. Each plan includes a cap of new listeners for the current billing cycle. Open **Settings → Subscription → Manage plan**, then the **Self-Serve** tab, for the plans and caps available to your workspace.

Plan names and caps come from that tab, not from this page.

## The usage meter

On **Settings → Subscription**, **New listeners on Soundlinks** shows used / cap for this cycle.

- Counted **once per Soundlink**
- Updated periodically — typically every couple of hours, not live
- Resets at the end of the billing period (the reset date in the app is UTC)

The sidebar **New listeners** bar is the same cap.

## When you reach the cap

The cap is a **soft limit**. New listeners stop being counted until the cycle resets. Existing Soundlinks keep working. Ads you run on Meta are unchanged.

To raise the cap for later cycles, upgrade on **Manage plan**. Upgrading starts a **new billing period**.

## Extra listeners

Some workspaces can buy extra listeners from **Settings → Subscription**. Those extras add to your plan cap until the current period ends. Because an upgrade starts a new period, unused extras are dropped.
