---
title: What counts
description: New Spotify listeners your Soundlinks bring in — counted once per Soundlink, not once per stream.
---

Your Self-Serve cap measures **new, real Spotify listeners** your Soundlinks bring in this billing cycle. Each person counts **once per Soundlink**, on the first cycle that Soundlink brings them in.

Repeat streams through the same Soundlink never add to the count. You are measured on new fans, not on how many times they play.

<Note>
  Listeners from Soundlink-funded ad campaigns are **not** counted here. Those
  campaigns are billed separately through the [credit wallet](/learn/wallet).
</Note>

## Same listener, later visit

Once a Soundlink has counted someone, every later visit on that Soundlink is a returning listener — no extra charge. That holds if they come back through your Meta ads, a playlist, or directly. What counts is their first listen on that Soundlink, not where later listens come from.

## More than one Soundlink

A person counts once **per Soundlink**. If they later discover a different Soundlink of yours, they can count once there too. Within one Soundlink they only ever count once.

## This meter is not the Soundlinks API

The usage row on **Settings → Subscription** is your **plan cap** for the billing cycle.

Soundlink metrics in the API (`new_listeners`, `listeners`) describe attributed listening for reporting. They are a different number. Do not treat an API total as the invoice or as the subscription meter. See [Soundlink metrics](/soundlink-metrics).
